# Tower Defense PVP 0.7.10 — experimental Windows patch

- Fresh matches and rematches start with **0 Gold**. Gather from deposits with your weapon to fund land and troop sends.
- Castles start with **20 points**; each ordinary arriving troop still removes one point.
- Class labels now use **Primal, Mystic, Sadist, Holy and Fiendish** throughout the character/tower catalogs.
- The main menu's **Tower Catalog** shows all 25 tower slots for inspection. Unnamed entries remain WIP; catalog-only entries cannot be placed. The five live tower prototypes retain their existing behavior and prices.

Windows only. Focused catalog/balance/castle checks and an exact local host/client fresh-match/rematch check passed. Real-device Steam/EOS and owner playtest acceptance remain open. New wiki abilities, costs and lifecycle rules are deferred.
