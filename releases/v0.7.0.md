# 0.7.0 — Economy, towers, spectators and two-player starts

Experimental Windows/macOS minor update collecting the 0.6.3–0.6.10 playtest patches.

- Two matching-version humans can join a normal room and both Ready to start. Four-player free-for-all remains available: all four should join before readying. Three humans wait for a fourth; vacant seats stay empty. Running matches reject late joins, and rematch needs every participating player's vote.
- Weapon attacks gather wood, stone, iron, gold and diamond from visible world reserves. Temporary yield is one resource unit per point of class attack damage. Normal range, cooldown, energy, life and visibility checks apply.
- Both Warrior and Wizard can build Archer, Crucible, Wolf Den, Church and Cemetary. Five stages total: T1/T2 cost 1 wood each, T3/T4 cost 1 iron each, Exhume costs 1 diamond. Defined Archer/Crucible effects work; undefined effects and the other three towers remain WIP. WIP tower stages are labelled before purchase and add no invented effect.
- G opens resource automation. Each type unlock costs 1 gold; visit and right-click an eligible individual node to buy its mine for 1 gold + 1 stone + 1 wood. Mines transfer 1 unit/second from shared reserves while the owner is living and the node is visible. No output catch-up while paused, no enemy wallet theft, and no new vision.
- Middle-drag orbits; right-click land, towers and resources opens context menus. Weapon, Army and Quick tower upgrade are the three hotbar slots. Army opens sends; troop upgrades remain WIP and spend nothing. Quick upgrade uses the same validated stage costs as tower menus. B/E/pickaxe and the universal build button are removed.
- Compact right-side HUD shows your resources/progression and public castle points; hero icon/level/health/energy are bottom-right. Stopping/reversing/turning preserves fractional movement-preview continuity, with the camera centered on the rendered hero.
- Castle elimination permits full-map viewing and WASD camera roaming, with no gameplay actions or shared vision. Enemy wallets/inventories/private mining progress remain private. Normal timed-death ghosts retain their existing own-side management/movement and restricted vision.
- Solo LOCAL PLAYTEST / 3 BOTS remains isolated to your computer. Mixed friend/bot rooms are not available yet. Ready and rematch still require your own input; leaving cleans up the three background clients.

Steam's explicit development App ID 480 and EOS guest rooms remain separate pools. This is an experimental GitHub download, not a production Steam release. The player host holds authority and is not a trusted competitive referee. Guest rooms are code-selected advertised lobbies. Non-host departure resets the remaining lobby; host loss ends the session. No queues/parties/teams, reconnect grace, migration or durable progression are claimed.

Minor-update checks and results are recorded before publication. Windows local regression is not real-device internet coverage. Mac is Universal Intel/Apple Silicon, macOS 12+, unsigned/unnotarized; static packaging does not prove Mac execution, peer connectivity or bot subprocess lifecycle. Real-device Steam/EOS, Mac runtime, long-soak, uncapped GPU and physical-wire checks remain open. Older releases/tags/assets stay fixed.
