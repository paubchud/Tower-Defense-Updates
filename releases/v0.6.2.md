# 0.6.2 — Local automated playtesting

Experimental pre-beta development build. Select Play -> Warrior/Wizard -> LOCAL PLAYTEST / 3 BOTS, then Ready. Your hero is human-controlled; three background local clients fill the other solo seats. No extra Steam/EOS accounts or port forwarding are needed for this mode.

Bots move, mine rock, buy land/build their own towers, send/upgrade troops and defend their incoming lane using normal multiplayer requests. They are simple test opponents, not polished competitive AI or a human tester. They cannot read hidden enemy wallets or authoritative match state. Rematch waits for your vote, then returns to fresh manual human ready-up. Leaving closes the owned bots; they also stop when their parent host exits.

Normal four-player Steam/EOS rooms remain separate and unchanged; local bots cannot join those rooms. This mode is loopback-only in development builds and does not verify real internet latency or provider connectivity. It runs four game processes, so it may require more resources than a normal single client.

Windows and Universal macOS ZIPs are experimental. Mac remains unsigned/unnotarized; Mac execution and local subprocess behavior require real Mac testing. No queues, party/team play, reconnect, host migration, persistence or new WIP content is enabled.
