## Approved wiki slice

- **1v1v1v1 only**, diagram-derived blocky volcano map with four bent 5-unit-wide paths, symmetric 3x3/5x5 plots and a 1-unit clear path buffer. Hero blockout is1x1x2; ordinary towers have3x3 bases. Diagram resources keep their authored positions/colors. Grey rock retains working stone harvesting; trees, iron, gold, diamond and volcano mechanics remain labeled inert WIP. Rail Gun/Crystal Ball5x5 concepts are catalogued, not active towers.
- One accepted send costs **1 gold total**, creates one Raider for every surviving enemy castle, never your own, and grants sending XP once. Hero ghosts still have surviving castles and receive troops. Future-troop upgrade costs1 sending XP. Atomic capacity/affordability/sequence checks prevent partial charges, duplicate sends or partly filled purchases.
- Each castle starts at **30 integer points**; each arriving troop removes exactly1, regardless of troop upgrade. A defeated castle eliminates that player while remaining castles continue. Last survivor wins; simultaneous final elimination can draw. Eliminated players cannot fight, transact, mine or reveal enemy terrain.
- **Primal** replaces Primate. Warrior:200HP,6speed,3flat defense,14damage. Wizard:100HP,4.2speed,1defense,21damage. These apply the approved L/M/H mapping against the provisional6-unit baseline; valid positive damage has a1 minimum after defense. Existing weapon range/cooldown/energy behavior remains provisional.
- Hero-only eligible troop/player killing blows award separate **Player XP**, never tower kills. Temporary values:1XP per kill,1XP per hero level,1 separate spendable level point per level. Levels do not automatically improve stats; spending points will not lower actual level. No bosses or level-point purchases are active yet. Sending XP remains independent.
- Land costs1gold; group towers cost1stone. Refunds cannot exceed paid prices. **G / In-game Upgrades** is an inactive tab; synergies have no effects. Existing rock mining, private equipment, hero-centered predicted movement, PvP, ghost/home respawn and per-enemy fog remain. Ghost movement/camera never adds enemy vision; visible enemy nodes expose shared reserves, not banked currency/XP/gear/harvest progress.
- All four rematch votes return to a clean lobby, then all four ready again. Results show your own private stock/progress, not opponents' hidden balances. Non-host departure resets remaining players to the lobby; host loss ends the session. No automated reconnect/forfeit or competitive authority trust is implied.
